# Physical view: CC-A to CC-B link

This view adds what the chapter diagram hides: the two CC-A to CC-B fibres follow physically distinct routes (north technical gallery, south outer facade), and each core has its own UPS.
Remaining common cause: both routes converge in the cable chamber under the courtyard, so one digger cuts both.
Also not independent: both UPS are assumed to be fed by the same mains supply.
Assumption: the routes, the chamber and the power feed are invented for this exercise, not taken from a real cabling plan.
